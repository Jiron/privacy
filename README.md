# privacy

The one legal page for all Jiron projects (jiron.dev and DiscordDevTools): privacy policy, legal notice and the DiscordDevTools terms of use.

Published by GitHub Pages at <https://jiron.github.io/privacy/privacy.html>. A push to `main` goes live in about a minute.

The projects link here and have no legal pages of their own, so a change to what a project processes has to be written in `privacy.html`, with a new date at the top.
